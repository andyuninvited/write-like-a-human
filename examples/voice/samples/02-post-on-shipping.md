We shipped the new returns flow on Tuesday. It took six weeks and three rewrites.

The first version asked customers why they were returning something. Nobody answered. The second version guessed the reason from the order. It guessed wrong a third of the time.

The version we shipped skips the question. You pick the item, print the label, and you're done. Support tickets about returns dropped by half in the first week.

I keep relearning this. The fastest form is the one with no fields.
