Leadership wanted to launch our referral program in March. I thought it would send new customers straight into our worst problem, which was late deliveries.

I pulled three months of complaints. Late deliveries were the top reason people cancelled, ahead of price. I brought that chart to the launch review and asked for three weeks to let operations fix the delivery windows first.

We got the three weeks. When the program launched, referral signups came in 30% above forecast, and the referred customers cancelled less often than any group we'd acquired that year. After that, delivery reliability became a launch checklist item for every growth program.
