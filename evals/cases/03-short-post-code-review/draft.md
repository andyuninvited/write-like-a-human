Here's the thing about code review that nobody talks about.

It's not about catching bugs. It's about shared understanding.

Last quarter, our team made one small change that unlocked a massive improvement: every pull request now needs a one-line "why" at the top. The results were incredible — review time dropped from two days to under one.

Why does this work? Because reviewers spend most of their time reverse-engineering intent. When you give them the why, they can focus on what matters: correctness, clarity, and maintainability.

In short, a single sentence can transform your entire review culture. Small changes, big impact.
